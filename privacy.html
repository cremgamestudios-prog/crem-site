Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

We have tried to describe exactly what the app does with your data, including the parts that are less flattering.

1. What we do not ask for

C-Rem Task Manager does not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, location, or a Google, Apple or social-media login. An account is a username and a password that you choose. If you pick your real name as a username, or write personal information into a task, that information is part of your content.

2. What the app stores

  • Account details: your username, your team's name, and passwords. Passwords (yours and your team's) are stored only as salted hashes, never as the password itself.
  • Your content: tasks, descriptions, subtasks, comments, labels, categories, due dates, assignments and attached images.
  • Activity: when you were last active (to show who is online), and when tasks were started and completed (to calculate streaks, work time, statistics and the team ranking). Everyone in your team can see this.
  • On your device: your preferences (theme, colors, view) and a local copy of your team's data, so the app keeps working through short connection drops.

3. How your data is protected

  • Data travels to our database over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and Google encrypts the data it stores in its data centers.
  • Passwords are hashed before they are stored.

Please note: C-Rem does not add its own end-to-end encryption to your tasks. Task content is stored in readable form in our database. That means C-Rem Studios, as the administrator of that database, could technically read it, and every member of your team can read and edit everything in the team. Please do not store passwords, financial or health information, or other sensitive secrets in tasks.

4. Services we rely on

  • Google Firebase (Firestore) stores your data and keeps it in sync between team members. When the app connects, Google processes technical data such as your IP address and device information under its own terms (Firebase privacy information).
  • GitHub hosts this website and the app's installers and updates. When you visit the site, download the app or check for an update, GitHub receives your IP address and request details (GitHub privacy statement).
  • Google Fonts is used by this website, so Google receives your IP address when you open it.
  • Voice dictation. If you use the microphone button, your speech is transcribed by the speech-recognition service built into the app's browser engine, which sends the audio to Google's servers. We do not receive or store the audio. Only the resulting text appears in the task box.

The app and this website do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

5. Sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent or trade your data, and we do not show ads. We share data only with the services listed above, as needed to run the app, or when the law requires it.

6. Retention and deletion

When you delete a task, it is removed from our database. Copies may remain on devices until they next sync, and Google may keep deleted data for a short period before it is purged from its systems. To have your team or account removed, contact us.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, or under the minimum age required in your country.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.

9. Contact
